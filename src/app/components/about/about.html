Design-minded developer

I'm a software developer based in Budapest. Most of my professional experience is in frontend development with Angular, including several years working for companies in the United Kingdom.

My favourite part of the job is the whole journey: figuring out how an app should look and feel, then building it pixel by pixel. I care about clean interfaces, accessibility and code that stays easy to read, test and extend.

Lately I've been expanding into the backend with NestJS, so I can take a product from first sketch to a fully working application.
